## Global Constraints

- Preserve synthetic repositories, profiles, screenshot fixtures and failed evidence. Do not clean sibling workspaces.
- No private organization/repository/path/PR/session text, titles, authors or credentials in the new shareable diagnostic export; no unsalted identifier hashes.
- No extra provider requests, scan demand or write authority solely for diagnostics. Preserve account isolation, budgets, backoff, freshness provenance and acknowledged-write fences.
- Preserve whole-job30-minute Rust limit, three race iterations and eight race-test threads; do not invent the old timeout's cause.
- Shared frontend requires mobile coverage; classify desktop commands on both mirrored surfaces; register companion-only commands locally in CLIENT_COMMANDS and the companion handler, outside the desktop surfaces; preserve older-host compatibility.
- Real enterprise laptop/iPhone17Pro testing occurs AFTER release by explicit owner decision; keep unfulfilled evidence tickets open and publish no synthetic-as-real claims.
- Versions remain0.1.0; annotatedv9.2.0 only on a commit with all check attempts successful; no protection bypass or burned-tag rerun.

## Review Focus

1. Secret-shaped text hidden by a fast-path false negative: differential masking tests include short ghp_a, mixed-case Bearer, assignments, Unicode, CRLF and overlapping spans.
2. An old phone/host or compact session-list hydration drops or misinterprets additive process/position data: real wire fixtures and mounted consumers must retain Unknown and honest unsupported navigation.
3. A scrubber response arrives after another seek/session change or append: generation tests pin the winner and explicit detached state, including EOF.
4. Diagnostic logging fails, overflows or sees private content: recorder tests prove user work continues, all state is bounded, loss is explicit and exported bytes exclude sentinels.
5. Missing/partial field observations masquerade as coverage, timing or convergence: real producer tests and the offline analyzer preserve unknowns, distinguish native versus mounted observations, and do not infer identity across epochs.
