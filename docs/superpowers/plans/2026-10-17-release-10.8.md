## Review Focus

- Initial/live/retained receipts and concurrent list observers must not miss changed facts or duplicate provider reads.

- Account/host changes and inactive/hidden consumers must not expose prior-owner data or create background request storms.
- Refresh failures must retain useful content with truthful status and preserve interaction state.
- Actual mounted consumers must observe invalidation, including unchanged search resubmission and unobserved-to-observed hook data.
